About

Hi, I’m Joey

I’m a Senior Product Designer at LinkedIn, leading design for premium experiences that reach hundreds of millions of professionals. My superpower is AI-native prototyping. I turn fuzzy model capabilities into clickable, opinionated product surfaces in hours, not weeks.

Over fifteen years I’ve shipped mobile systems at the frontier: founding-era LinkedIn iOS, the first FAA-approved drone delivery at Google X’s Wing, and Cannes Gold Lion work at Studio XO with Lady Gaga. I’m also the author of Think. Prompt. Create. (2026), a playbook for building software with AI.

Stanford d.School alum and guest lecturer, Forbes 30 Under 30. Lives in the Bay Area with his mini Goldendoodle, Toshi.
