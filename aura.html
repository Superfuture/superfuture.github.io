Aura

An ambient AI agent for your phone.

A design exploration of the hardest questions in agentic products: how much it can do, what it can see, and when it should ask.

Scroll

Apps wait for taps. Agents act.

01 · Ambient

It lives on the lock screen.

No app to open. Aura surfaces only what matters, the moment it matters, and fades back to glass when it doesn't.

02 · Trust

You set how far it can go.

One dial, three postures. Suggest only, confirm each action, or let it run. Autonomy is a setting, never an assumption.

The hard part isn't ability. It's trust.

03 · Human in the loop

Nothing ships without a yes.

Before any action with consequences, Aura shows exactly what it will do, in plain language, with one tap to approve, edit or hold.

04 · Transparency

It shows its work.

An agent you can't inspect is an agent you can't trust. Aura keeps a live, legible ledger of what it sees, remembers and acts on.

05 · Reasoning

You can watch it think.

When Aura works, it narrates, step by step, in real time, so the moment it heads the wrong way you can catch it.

06 · Honesty

When unsure, it asks.

The bravest thing an agent can do is admit doubt. Aura surfaces its uncertainty as a choice, not a silent guess you discover later.

Designed to be felt, not managed.
